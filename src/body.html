Privacy

There are no ads, analytics or tracking. In the Android, iPhone and standalone web versions, your choices, saved quotes and written quotes are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer. In the claude.ai version they are stored in a private space for your account. A quote leaves the app only when you copy, share or back it up. Erase all my data removes what the app has stored; delete any backup files or copies yourself.

"Paraphrase" means the line is put in plain words and is not a direct quotation. Check the cited source before you quote it elsewhere.
