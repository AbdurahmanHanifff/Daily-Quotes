# Privacy policy: Daily Quote Feed

_Draft. Add the publisher's name, a contact address and a date before publishing._

- No ads, analytics, tracking or accounts in the Android, iPhone and standalone web versions.
- Your belief system choices, saved quotes, written quotes, streak and settings are stored on your device. The developer does not receive them. Choices can reveal religious beliefs; they stay on the device.
- Your phone's own backup service may include app data, depending on your phone settings.
- The claude.ai version stores this data in a private per-account space in the page's database, under Anthropic's terms.
- A web host serving the standalone web version may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses.
- Data leaves the app only when you copy, share or back it up.
- Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push notification server is used.
- Retention and deletion: data stays until you use Settings > Erase all my data, or remove the app or its site data. Delete exported backups yourself.
